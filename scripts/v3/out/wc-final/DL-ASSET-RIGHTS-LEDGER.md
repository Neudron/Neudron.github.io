# DL-ASSET-RIGHTS-LEDGER — downloaded art + turnbattle jar (2026-11-20)

Read-only rights record for the 14 sprite/sheet downloads and
`turnbattle-2.0.29.jar` that were staged into `img/act4/fan/**` on branch
`dl-assets` at commit d89c5ab. Evidence gathered from
`scripts/v3/out/wc-final/DL-RIGHTS-AUDIT.md` and
`scripts/v3/out/wc-final/TURNBATTLE-JAR-ANALYSIS.md` §1.

**No row in this ledger is CLEARED.** Every fan row is
OWNER-ACCEPTED-FAN-USE (NOT CLEARED): the owner accepted the risk of
publishing non-commercial fan art, and that decision is not a licence and not
a redistribution right. No written permission from Toby Fox, The Funkin' Crew
or Pixelcite exists in this repository.

## 4. Owner decision, stated plainly

1. All 14 sprite/sheet downloads are rips of copyrighted game art. The source
   host states it "cannot license any of them to you" and "does not own" them.
2. Deltarune is © Toby Fox 2018-2026, all rights reserved. FNF art is
   proprietary and "may not be publicly distributed for free or for profit by
   anyone but the copyright owner".
3. The owner has accepted the risk of publishing this art on a non-commercial
   fan site. That is recorded as OWNER-ACCEPTED-FAN-USE for each group above.
   It is not a licence, not approval, and not clearance.
4. Public GitHub Pages publication is a formal publication. Whether that
   breaches the sources' cautionary language is a legal judgement these pages
   do not settle; the language is cautionary, not a court ruling.
5. The jar is not shipped. Its code may be re-implemented under MIT with the
   notice retained. Its audio and textures stay out of the repo entirely.
6. Nothing in this ledger may be relabelled CLEARED. Doing so would misstate
   the rightsholders' position and the owner's own decision.
